TextLift privacy policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

TextLift helps you capture and copy text from webpage elements. Its publisher is the maintainer of the TextLift repository.

Information processed

When you activate TextLift, it reads the text or available label of the webpage element under your pointer and displays it in a preview on your device. It can read ordinary form values, except password fields. The text you choose may contain personal information, communications, financial details, health information, or other information already present on the page. TextLift processes that content only to show the preview and perform your requested copy.

The extension receives information about the current browser tab to run its picker there. It does not maintain a browsing history, monitor other tabs, or use page content to build a profile of you.

Local processing and retention

TextLift does not send webpage text, form values, tab information, or clipboard content to the publisher or to external servers. It has no analytics, advertising, user account, or remote executable code.

Preview text is held temporarily in memory while the picker is open. Closing the picker removes its preview. For a copy operation, the selected text passes to a temporary extension clipboard document on the same device; its field is cleared after copying and the document is closed. TextLift does not create a saved copy history.

Copied text remains in your system clipboard until you or your operating system replace or clear it. Clipboard history, synchronization, other applications, and the website itself are governed by their own settings and policies. TextLift never reads your clipboard.

Use and sharing

TextLift uses locally processed text only for its capture, preview, editing, and copy features. The publisher does not receive, sell, or share that text, use it for advertising, or use it for creditworthiness or lending decisions.

Permissions

  • activeTab grants temporary access to the page where you invoke the extension.
  • scripting adds the picker to that page.
  • clipboardWrite lets TextLift write the text you explicitly choose to the clipboard.
  • offscreen provides a temporary extension document for copying, including on HTTP pages.

Your choices

You control when the picker starts, which element is captured, and what is copied. You can edit the preview, select only part of it, or close it with Escape. To remove clipboard content, use your operating system's clipboard controls. You can disable or uninstall TextLift in Chrome's extension settings.

Support and changes

Privacy questions can be submitted through GitHub Issues. Do not include sensitive webpage text or clipboard content in a public issue. If you voluntarily submit an issue, GitHub processes that submission under its own privacy policy.

Material changes to TextLift's data practices will be reflected in an updated privacy policy and extension version.
